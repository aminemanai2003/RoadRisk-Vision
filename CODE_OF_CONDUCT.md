# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience or viewpoint.

Be respectful, discuss evidence rather than people, accept constructive review,
and consider the real-world safety and privacy impact of contributions. Do not
publish another person's footage, location or personal information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained
disruption, sexualized attention and publishing private information. Maintainers
may edit, remove or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose conduct is harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to `amine.manai@esprit.tn`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
