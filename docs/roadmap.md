# Roadmap

## v0.1

- Versioned local pipeline, CLI/dashboard, privacy-safe artifacts and mocks.
- HybridNets + YOLOX adapters, tracking, optional geometry and risk events.
- Windows/Linux contributor CI without weight or network use during tests.

## After v0.1

- Licensed evaluation fixtures and RTX 2050 benchmark report.
- Interactive point selection and checkerboard lens calibration.
- Validated lane-departure logic and improved temporal uncertainty.
- Dataset tooling and modernized HybridNets training under `training/`.

Android live inference, vehicle actuation and insurance pricing are not planned.
