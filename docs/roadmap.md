# Roadmap

## v0.1 - released

- Versioned local pipeline, CLI/dashboard, privacy-safe artifacts and mocks.
- HybridNets + YOLOX adapters, tracking, optional geometry and risk events.
- Windows/Linux contributor CI without weight or network use during tests.
- Licensed synthetic fixtures and an RTX 2050 benchmark report.
- Interactive four-point camera calibration.
- Evidence-gated lane-departure events and actuarial exposure reporting.

## After v0.1

- Lens-profile use inside the analysis pipeline (checkerboard estimation has
  landed; see `docs/calibration.md`), and improved temporal uncertainty.
- Broader licensed road-condition validation datasets.
- Dataset tooling and modernized HybridNets training under `training/`.

Android live inference, vehicle actuation and insurance pricing are not planned.
