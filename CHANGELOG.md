# Changelog

All notable project changes are documented here. RoadRisk Vision follows
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## 0.1.0 - 2026-08-12

- Added offline H.264/H.265 phone-video normalization with rotation, VFR and
  privacy-safe audio/metadata removal.
- Added self-contained HybridNets D3 and YOLOX-S CUDA/CPU inference, checksum-
  locked model downloads and two-stage ByteTrack association.
- Added optional four-point camera calibration, distance/TTC suppression rules,
  lane-departure evidence and deterministic temporal risk events.
- Added GPX/CSV telemetry alignment, versioned artifacts and actuarial exposure
  reports that explicitly exclude claims and premium modelling.
- Added the Typer CLI, Streamlit analysis/calibration/review dashboard, FFmpeg
  diagnostics, licensed synthetic fixtures and hermetic CI tests.
- Published an RTX 2050 4 GB benchmark: 300/300 frames at 1080p/30, 4.94x source
  duration, 2.53 GB peak process RAM and 0.203 GB peak allocated VRAM.

[Semantic Versioning]: https://semver.org/
